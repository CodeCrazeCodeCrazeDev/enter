# Prioritized Return-On-Investment (ROI) Roadmap

## 1. Evaluation Methodology

Engineering initiatives are evaluated and ranked based on a composite **Return on Engineering Investment (ROEI)** score calculated as:

$$\text{ROEI} = \frac{\text{Capability Impact} \times \text{Reliability Multiplier}}{\text{Engineering Effort (Person-Weeks)} \times \text{System Complexity Risk}}$$
