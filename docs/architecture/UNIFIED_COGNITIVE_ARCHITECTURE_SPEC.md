# Specification: Unified Cognitive Architecture

## 1. System Overview & Architectural Mandate

The Autonomous Entrepreneurial Operating System (Cognitive OS) integrates **Research OS**, **EIOS**, **EOS**, **AEAN**, and **APODEX** into a single, cohesive, 4-layered cognitive operating system.

### 4-Layer System Taxonomy:
1. **Layer 1: Research OS (Scientific Research Layer)**
   - Responsible for scientific discovery, automated literature review over 300+ research papers, hypothesis generation, reproducible code synthesis, and statistical validation.
2. **Layer 2: EIOS / EOS (Execution & Sensing Layer)**
   - Responsible for environment sensing, active inference Expected Free Energy (EFE) calculation, causal interventions (do-calculus), and 13 business lifecycle feedback loops.
3. **Layer 3: AEAN (Cognitive Intelligence Layer)**
   - Responsible for multi-agent swarm coordination, Graph-of-Thought (GoT) planning, Ebbinghaus memory decay retention, active learning, and skill execution.
4. **Layer 4: APODEX (Decision Engine & Autonomous Execution Platform Layer)**
   - Responsible for the 14-Layer Computational Architecture of Entrepreneurship, Advanced Kelly Criterion capital allocation, game-theoretic deceptive signal detection, decision fatigue tracking, and safety governance.

## 2. Core Operational Subsystems

### A. Scientific Research & Hypothesis Generator (Layer 1)
- **Module**: `apodex.ai_eos.research.research_os.ResearchOS`
- **Capabilities**:
  - `conduct_literature_review(query)`: Dynamically queries the 300-paper corpus (`AI_EOS_RESEARCH_DB.yaml` & `ALPHA_ALGO_100_NEW_RESEARCH.yaml`).
  - `generate_falsifiable_hypothesis(domain)`: Generates structured hypotheses with explicit null/alternative states.
  - `export_validated_hypothesis_to_kernel()`: Hands off validated hypotheses to Layer 2 EIOS Kernel.

### B. Active Inference Operating Kernel (Layer 2)
- **Module**: `apodex.arcs.kernel.kernel.EIOSKernel`
- **Capabilities**:
  - Active Inference EFE routing: $\text{EFE} = \text{Pragmatic Value} + \text{Epistemic Information Gain}$.
  - Pearl's Do-Calculus Causal Interventions $P(Y | do(X))$.
  - Real-time opportunity anomaly sensing (`sense_opportunity_anomalies`).

### C. Swarm Cognitive Intelligence & Memory (Layer 3)
- **Module**: `apodex.cognition.brain.CognitiveBrain` & `apodex.aean`
- **Capabilities**:
  - Graph-of-Thought (GoT) dynamic thought branching and scoring.
  - Epistemic Ebbinghaus Memory Decay for context management.
  - Swarm Debate consensus with game-theoretic sycophancy mitigation.

### D. Computational Architecture of Entrepreneurship (Layer 4)
- **Module**: `apodex.ai_eos.intelligence.computational_architecture.ComputationalArchitectureOfEntrepreneurship`
- **Capabilities**:
  - End-to-end multi-objective Pareto optimization across 14 computational layers (Layer 1 Reality Substrate through Layer 14 Autonomous AI Entrepreneurship).
  - Advanced Kelly Criterion capital sizing with drawdown guards.
