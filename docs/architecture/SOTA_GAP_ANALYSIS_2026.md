## Key Differentiators & Strategic Moats

1. **Integrated First-Principles Scientific Engine**:
   - Existing agentic frameworks rely on trial-and-error without statistical rigor. The Unified OS integrates `ResearchOS`, which performs statistical power analysis, $t$-tests, and multiplicity corrections ($p < 0.05$) before promoting hypotheses to production.

2. **Causal Reasoning over Correlation**:
   - Competitive platforms use simple heuristic prompting or reward functions. The Unified OS executes Structural Causal Models (SCMs) and Pearl's do-calculus to model explicit interventions ($P(Y | do(X))$), preventing spurious correlation errors during long-horizon execution.

3. **Multi-Timescale Economic Realism**:
   - The Unified OS integrates financial, market, and treasury loops with Kelly Criterion sizing, ensuring autonomous operations adhere to economic constraints and ROI viability.
