# SOTA Gap Analysis (2026 AI Systems Landscape)

## Comparison Framework & Benchmarks

The Unified Cognitive Operating System is benchmarked against state-of-the-art AI systems across six primary capability axes:

1. **Long-Horizon Autonomous Execution** (e.g. Devin, SWE-agent)
2. **Scientific Hypothesis & Research Discovery** (e.g. AI Scientist, AlphaFold/AlphaZero paradigms)
3. **Entrepreneurial & Business Reasoning** (e.g. EOS, Market Anomaly Engine)
4. **Multi-Agent Coordination & Swarm Bidding** (e.g. ChatDev, MetaGPT, HiveMind Token Economy)
5. **Causal Reasoning & Active Inference Sensing** (e.g. Active Inference EFE, Pearl Do-Calculus)
6. **Continuous Learning & Skill Flywheels** (e.g. Voyager, Self-Improving Agents)
