## The Master Meta-Question Formalization

> **"What is the complete computational architecture of entrepreneurship—from sensing changes in the external world, to discovering opportunities, validating them, designing businesses, acquiring customers, allocating capital, scaling organizations, and continuously learning—and how can each component be formalized into algorithms, feedback loops, decision systems, and autonomous AI agents?"**

### Systemic Solution Framework
1. **Sensing & Discovery:** High-dimensional state space $\mathcal{S}$ is continuously scanned for Kullback-Leibler (KL) divergence anomalies between supply $Q(s)$ and demand $P(s)$ distributions.
2. **Causal Problem Formulation:** Problems are represented as Directed Acyclic Graphs (DAGs) under Pearl's Structural Causal Models (SCM) $Y = f(X, U)$, separating downstream symptoms from latent root causes.
3. **Active Inference Decision Making:** Actions $\pi$ are selected by minimizing Expected Free Energy (EFE):
   $$G(\pi) = - \text{Pragmatic Value} - \gamma \cdot \text{Epistemic Information Gain}$$
4. **Execution & Scaling:** Products are minimal Kolmogorov complexity realizations satisfying core Jobs-To-Be-Done (JTBD). Marketing and customer acquisition follow scale-free graph cascades and Push-Pull-Inertia-Anxiety switching dynamics.
5. **Continuous Triple-Loop Learning:** Operational errors backpropagate across 3 loops: Loop 1 adjusts parameters, Loop 2 rewrites operational playbooks, and Loop 3 performs meta-evolutionary code rewrites.

## Detailed Formalization Across All 14 Layers

### Layer 1: Reality Substrate
* **Fundamental Principle:** Low-entropy energy and capital transformation into high-utility outputs subject to thermodynamic, Shannon information, and temporal limits.
* **Human Psychology vs Optimization:** Human psychology defines terminal subjective utility functions $U_{term}$, whereas resource conversion paths are pure computational optimization problems.
* **Automation Boundary:** Terminal human meaning and subjective arbitration cannot be automated. Algorithmic state-space search, scheduling, and causal inference are fully automated.

### Layer 2: Opportunity Discovery
* **State Space Search:** Continuous scanning for regions where market supply $Q$ and demand $P$ exhibit high KL divergence: $D_{KL}(P \parallel Q) = \sum P(x) \log \frac{P(x)}{Q(x)}$.
* **Weak Signal Detection:** Low-frequency, high-variance z-score anomaly detection across unstructured temporal data feeds.
* **Novelty Generation:** Cross-domain conceptual tensor products combining disparate semantic matrices.

### Layer 3: Problem Discovery
* **Decomposition:** Directed acyclic causal graph $X \to Z \to Y$. Root causes $X$ have zero parents; symptoms $Y$ have zero children.
* **Ignore Criteria:** Evaluated via Expected Value of Information Gain (EVIG):
  $$\text{EVIG} + \Delta U_{\text{solve}} < C_{\text{investigate}}$$

### Layer 4: Decision Making
* **Knightian Uncertainty Resolution:** Active Inference EFE policy selection balancing pragmatic cash flow utility with epistemic curiosity.
* **Rapid Idea Termination:** Sequential Probability Ratio Test (SPRT) under asymmetric decision boundaries:
  $$\Lambda_n = \sum_{i=1}^n \ln \frac{P(x_i | H_1)}{P(x_i | H_0)}$$
* **Confirmation Bias Mitigation:** Adversarial negative-bias search agents.

### Layer 5: Opportunity Evaluation
* **Expected Value:** Risk-discounted expected cash flow simulation: $EV = \text{TAM} \cdot \text{Margin} \cdot P(\text{success}) \cdot (1 - \text{Risk})$.
* **Timing & Option Value:** Real Option to defer investment valued against first-mover advantage.
* **Abandonment Trigger:** $G_{\text{continue}} > G_{\text{pivot}} + C_{\text{switch}}$.

### Layer 6: Product Creation
* **Complexity Minimization:** Regularization penalty $K(P)$ on feature space, selecting the minimum viable complexity required to satisfy the customer's core Job-To-Be-Done (JTBD).
* **Learning-First Design:** Every feature release acts as an epistemic probe updating the system's world model.

### Layer 7: Customer Understanding
* **Customer Active Inference:** Customers minimize cognitive and financial free energy.
* **Switching Dynamics:**
  $$P(\text{switch}) = \sigma(\text{Push} + \text{Pull} - \text{Inertia} - \text{Anxiety})$$

### Layer 8: Marketing
* **Attention Spread:** Dynamic information cascades on scale-free social graphs.
* **Authority & Positioning:** Positioning coordinates projected into low-dimensional customer perception spaces; authority computed via PageRank over domain belief networks.

### Layer 9: Sales
* **Transaction Mechanics:** Financial capital exchanged for risk reduction.
* **Automation Boundary:** $ACV < \$5k$ routes to self-serve automated funnels; $ACV \ge \$5k$ routes to multi-stakeholder enterprise sales.

### Layer 10: Growth
* **Sub-quadratic Network Effects:** Beckstrom/Zipf scaling $V \propto N \log_2 N$.
* **Slowdown Controls:** Intentional growth throttling triggered when growth rate exceeds operational support throughput capacity.

### Layer 11: Competition
* **Rivalry Modeling:** Iterated game theory utility optimization.
* **Moat Durability:** Composite score across switching costs, network density, and cost advantages.

### Layer 12: Organizational Design
* **Hiring Triggers:** Lagrange shadow price of bottleneck constraint exceeding market wage ($\lambda_{\text{constraint}} > W_{\text{market}}$).
* **Coase Boundaries:** Centralize internally if internal coordination cost $C_{\text{int}} < C_{\text{ext}}$ external transaction cost; otherwise delegate or outsource.

### Layer 13: Meta-Learning
* **Triple-Loop Learning:**
  - Loop 1: Active parameter adjustments.
  - Loop 2: Operational playbook rewrite.
  - Loop 3: Meta-code evolutionary rewriting.

### Layer 14: Autonomous AI Entrepreneurship
* **Master Orchestrator:** Implemented in `FourteenLayerEntrepreneurialEngine` in `apodex/ai_eos/intelligence/fourteen_layer_engine.py`, driving continuous, autonomous enterprise lifecycle loops across all 14 layers.
