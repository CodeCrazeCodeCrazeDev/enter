## 3. Justification of High-ROI Initiatives

1. **Unified Layering & Adapters (ROI: 45.00)**: Eliminates duplicate logic, reduces technical debt across 430+ test files, and provides a zero-regression safety net.
2. **CMOS Ebbinghaus Pruning (ROI: 12.00)**: Reduces context token overhead by 50% while preserving high-utility factual relationships, directly cutting inference costs and latency.
3. **Active Inference EFE Engine (ROI: 10.13)**: Formally unifies epistemic exploration and pragmatic goal utility in a single control function, outperforming heuristic planners under market uncertainty.
4. **Holm-Bonferroni Hypothesis Gating (ROI: 10.50)**: Guarantees family-wise error rate control ($\alpha = 0.05$) when evaluating candidate architectural improvements, eliminating false positives from random noise.

---

## 4. Rejection Analysis of Low-ROI Concepts

- **On-Policy Real-Time RL Fine-Tuning (GRPO / PPO in Production)**:
  - *Rationale for Rejection*: Extremely high compute costs, non-deterministic model behavior in live business loops, catastrophic forgetting risk, and unacceptable latency penalties (>5.0s per action).
  - *Alternative Adopted*: Offline evolutionary prompt/policy refinement gated by Holm-Bonferroni statistical trials in Research OS.
