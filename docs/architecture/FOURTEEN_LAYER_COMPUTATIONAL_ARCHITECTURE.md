# Fourteen-Layer Computational Architecture of Entrepreneurship

## Overview

This specification documents the first-principles computational decomposition of entrepreneurship into a 14-layer operating system architecture, implemented in executable Python in `apodex/ai_eos/intelligence/fourteen_layer_engine.py`.

---

## Layer Decomposition

### Layer 1: Reality Substrate
- **Fundamental Principle**: Entrepreneurship is free energy minimization over market state space under extreme uncertainty.
- **Decomposition**: Human Psychology (trust, narrative, empathy) vs Optimization Problems (unit economics, CAC/LTV, pricing, allocation).
- **Automation Bounds**:
  - *Non-automatable*: Human physical empathy, subjective baseline preference setting, ultimate ethical values.
  - *Automatable*: Weak signal sensing, causal inference, cohort financial simulation, capital allocation, active inference.

### Layer 2: Opportunity Discovery
- **Continuous State Space Search**: Active inference filtering raw environmental signals via Signal-to-Noise Ratio ($SNR \ge 2.0$).
- **Combinatorial Synthesis**: Cross-domain observation fusion ($A \times B$).
- **Trend Velocity**: Second-derivative growth acceleration evaluation ($0.4 \cdot g + 0.6 \cdot a$).

### Layer 3: Problem Discovery
- **Stated vs Real Problem**: Decomposing causal Directed Acyclic Graphs (DAGs) to isolate latent root cause nodes from surface symptoms.
- **Ignore Criteria**: TAM $< \$1\text{M}$ or unresolvable structural unit economics cost floor.

### Layer 4: Decision Making
- **Expected Free Energy (EFE)**:
  $$\text{EFE} = -\ln(P(\text{success})) - w_{\text{curiosity}} \cdot (\text{Entropy}_{\text{prior}} - \text{Entropy}_{\text{simulated}})$$
- **Kill Gates**: Hard decision gates terminating ideas when $P(\text{success}) < 0.15$ or $\text{EFE} > 2.0$.

### Layer 5: Opportunity Evaluation
- **Financial Value & CVaR**: Expected Value $\text{EV} = P \cdot \text{TAM} - (1-P) \cdot \text{Loss}$, adjusted for Conditional Value at Risk (CVaR).
- **Real Options**: Black-Scholes paradigm evaluating Option to Wait vs Option to Strike based on market entropy.

### Layer 6: Product Creation
- **MVP Boundary**: Pareto filtering features based on JTBD impact vs build complexity ($\text{ROI} = \text{Impact} / \text{Complexity} \ge 1.0$).
- **Learning Velocity**: Information gain (bits) per dollar-day spent.

### Layer 7: Customer Understanding
- **Switching Barrier**: Sigmoid probability model over net value delta, brand trust, and switching costs.
- **Churn Hazard**: Analytical hazard rate modeling customer usage frequency and support friction.

### Layer 8: Marketing
- **Epidemic Viral Diffusion**: Viral coefficient $K = \text{Invites} \cdot \text{Conversion Rate}$. $K > 1.0$ triggers compounding viral acquisition.
- **Channel Attribution**: Multi-channel yield normalization.

### Layer 9: Sales
- **Sales Touch Threshold**:
  - $\text{ACV} \ge \$10,000$: Field Enterprise Sales.
  - $\$2,000 \le \text{ACV} < \$10,000$: Inside Hybrid Sales.
  - $\text{ACV} < \$2,000$: Fully Automated Self-Serve.
- **Objection Resolution**: Causal graph mapping customer objections to optimal counter-positioning scripts.

### Layer 10: Growth
- **Network Effects**: Metcalfe's Law ($N^2$) vs Reed's Law ($2^N$).
- **Net Revenue Retention (NRR)**: Tracking expansion vs churn over baseline MRR.

### Layer 11: Competition
- **Moat Durability**: Composite score combining network density, switching costs, and proprietary technological advantage.
- **Pivot Trigger**: Strategic inflection point detection when market share delta $< -20\%$ and moat score $< 0.30$.

### Layer 12: Organizational Design
- **Hiring Trigger**: Capacity utilization $> 85\%$ and Revenue/Employee $\ge \$150\text{k}$.
- **Task Routing**: Routing tasks to Centralized Founder Core, Automated AI Agent Pods, or Delegated Team Pods.

### Layer 13: Meta-Learning
- **Decision Quality**: Brier score calculation measuring probabilistic forecast accuracy over real-world outcomes.
- **Failure-to-Knowledge Conversion**: Structural causal model re-fitting from post-mortem experiment failures.

### Layer 14: AI Entrepreneurship
- **Task Taxonomy**: Classification into Deterministic Algorithms, Probabilistic Reasoning, Causal Inference, Creative Generation, or Human Judgment.
- **Autonomous Resource Allocation**: Proportional allocation of capital, compute, time, and talent across prioritized opportunities.
- **Unified Master Orchestrator**: `FourteenLayerEntrepreneurialEngine` coordinating continuous sensing, evaluation, decision execution, and self-learning loops.
