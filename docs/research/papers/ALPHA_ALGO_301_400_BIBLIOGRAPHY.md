## Executive Summary of Extracted Transferable Principles (Papers 301-400)

From this 100-paper corpus, five core architectural and algorithmic improvements have been extracted and integrated into AlphaAlgo:

1. **Hawkes Non-Gaussian AST Call Depth Check (Paper 302 / 383):** In `CodeRewriteEngine`, enforces recursive AST function call depth bounds during self-referential mutation verification to prevent stack overflow and non-Gaussian instability.
2. **MAP-Elites Island Population Re-Sorting & Parameter Safeguards (Paper 382 / 389 / 390):** In `GeneticWorkflowOptimizer`, immediately re-sorts island populations upon elite genome insertion/migration and enforces parameter boundaries to guarantee quality diversity.
3. **Clipped Trajectory Advantage Margin Bounds (Paper 341 / 342 / 354):** In `SFTPreferenceCollector`, applies smooth advantage margin clipping (`tanh` bounded range `[-5.0, 5.0]`) to prevent gradient explosion during DPO preference tuning.
4. **Budget-Decayed Expected Free Energy Task Routing (Paper 327 / 377):** In `LearnableRoutingGateDispatcher`, incorporates budget exponential decay into Active Inference Expected Free Energy scoring to ensure smooth Pareto-optimal agent transition when compute budgets decline.
5. **Multi-Field Semantic Research Querying (Paper 392):** In `ResearchOS`, extends `conduct_literature_review` from static dummy outputs to real multi-field semantic querying over registered paper corpus principles across categories, descriptions, paper titles, and target subsystems.
