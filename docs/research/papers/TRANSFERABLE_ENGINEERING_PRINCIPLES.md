## 1. Active Inference, Expected Free Energy & Epistemic Curiosity
- **Pillars:** Friston (2010), Parr & Friston (2017/2019), Tschantz et al. (2020), Millidge et al. (2021).
- **Core Principle:** Action and perception selection minimizes Variational Free Energy ($VFE$) while epistemic value in Expected Free Energy ($EFE$) minimizes information surprise.
- **Subsystem Target Integration:**
  - **EIOS Kernel:** Active inference anomaly sensing measures pragmatic and epistemic surprise vectors to trigger targeted environmental sensing.
  - **ResearchOS:** Epistemic curiosity metrics drive hypothesis generation in high-uncertainty regions.

## 2. Multi-Agent Consensus, Game Theory & Swarm Intelligence
- **Pillars:** Foerster et al. (2016/2018), Sandholm & Varian (2020), Perez & Conitzer (2023), Du et al. (2023).
- **Core Principle:** Sycophancy-proof multi-agent debate and counterfactual credit assignment eliminate free-riding and collusion in distributed agent swarms.
- **Subsystem Target Integration:**
  - **AEAN HiveMind:** Token bidding registry incorporates counterfactual credit metrics and peer review score penalties to resolve multi-agent task execution conflicts.
  - **EOS Engine:** Game-theoretic equilibrium solvers evaluate competitive market positioning before promoting strategic business decisions.

## 3. Structural Causal Inference & Counterfactual Attribution
- **Pillars:** Pearl (2009), Peters et al. (2017), Arjovsky et al. (2019), Schölkopf et al. (2021).
- **Core Principle:** $Do$-calculus interventions and Invariant Risk Minimization ($IRM$) disentangle causal mechanisms from spurious observational correlations.
- **Subsystem Target Integration:**
  - **EIOS Kernel:** Causal DAGs audit environmental anomalies to ensure interventional invariance across regime shifts.
  - **AEAN Architecture:** Counterfactual execution trace analysis isolates sub-agent failure nodes without halting the global multi-agent execution pipeline.

## 4. Reinforcement Learning Alignment, DPO & Process Supervision
- **Pillars:** Rafailov et al. (2023), Lightman et al. (2023), Ouyang et al. (2022), DeepSeek-AI (2024).
- **Core Principle:** Direct Preference Optimization ($DPO$) and Process Reward Models ($PRM$) align multi-step agent trajectories with verifiable execution goals while preventing reward collapse.
- **Subsystem Target Integration:**
  - **ResearchOS:** On-policy advantage estimation packages trajectory steps into chosen/rejected preference pairs for offline policy tuning.
  - **AEAN Agent Workflows:** Process-level verifiers validate step-by-step agent execution outputs against formal schema invariants.

## 5. Quality Diversity, MAP-Elites & Island Evolutionary Search
- **Pillars:** Mouret & Clune (2015), Real et al. (2020/2024), Romera-Paredes et al. (2024), Whitley et al. (1999).
- **Core Principle:** MAP-Elites quality-diversity search and isolated island migration maintain behavioral diversity while discovering novel execution heuristics.
- **Subsystem Target Integration:**
  - **AEAN Workflow Optimizer:** Island-based population mutation and MAP-Elites routing enable continuous self-evolution of prompt templates and parameter configurations.
  - **ResearchOS Compiler:** Genetic program mutation synthesizes specialized sub-agent execution workflows.
