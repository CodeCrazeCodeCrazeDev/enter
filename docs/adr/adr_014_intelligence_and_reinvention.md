# ADR 014: Customer, Competitive, Growth, and Reinvention Intelligence

## Status
Approved

## Context
Legacy competitive and growth tracking relies on simple set overlap comparisons, ignoring weighted feature priorities, multi-touch channel attribution, and structural regime changes.

## Decision
We deploy:
1. Multi-touch attribution modeling and Bayesian CRO funnel experiments in the Growth Engine.
2. Bayesian Surprise update algorithms in the Reinvention Engine to detect structural market pivots.

## Consequences
- **Pros:**
  - Optimizes GTM spend with high precision.
  - Prevents competitive disruption via timely, structured self-adaptation.
- **Cons:**
  - Requires integration of complex multi-source channel events.
